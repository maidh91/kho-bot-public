What changed the answer

Costs matter

Recorded spreads were the main comparison. When the fifteen primary choices were rerun with assumed USD 0.20 per ounce slippage and USD 7 per lot per side commission, only one still met its preset’s original profit and drawdown conditions. Missing a condition did not always mean losing money.

More activity was mixed

Faster entries helped some combinations, while others gained little or breached their drawdown limit. Weekly profit targets and early closing also helped some tests and hurt others. These findings describe the tested combinations, not a general rule.

How to read these numbers

Research, not a forecast

  • All dates shown here were available to the research search. There is no untouched forward test.
  • Results use simulated fills and account rules; they do not prove broker execution or future returns.
  • Price bars cannot reveal every within-minute fill order or exact liquidation price.

Preset objectives

  • Stable: target drawdown at or below 20%, full return above 300%, and positive returns in each rolling 36-month window.
  • Dynamic: target drawdown at or below 40% and full return above 2,000%, with automatic setup selection among the typical choices.
  • Max: target drawdown at or below 60% and full return above 4,000%.
